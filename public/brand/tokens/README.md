# Pay-token marks

The two tokens Vestail accepts as payment, as their own marks rather than as
a dollar sign and a stack of bars drawn by hand.

Both are committed exactly as published — no recolouring, no cropping. The
Solana mark comes on a black square, which the interface shows inside a round
mask, the way that mark is normally set.

These are the trademarks of Circle and the Solana Foundation. They appear here
to name the token a buyer is paying with, which is what a token mark is for;
nothing here implies endorsement or any relationship with either.
