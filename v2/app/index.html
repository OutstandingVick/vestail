      01 The problem

      The same asset isn't the same for everyone.

      Whether you can own something depends on where it is, where you're from, and which version you're buying. The rules are scattered across statutes, regulators and issuer terms, so most people buy blind and find out at the worst moment.

        03 Three verdicts

        Not yes or no. The middle is where people get hurt.

        A two-state answer has to lie about the large middle: assets anyone can buy but can't redeem, collect on or sell without clearing a gate. Vestail gives it its own name.

        Can own

        The rules permit someone like you to hold it, with no further gate.

        What Vestail does
        Shows you where to buy it.

        Conditional

        Allowed, but behind a licence, cap, approval or KYC check, often one you only meet at exit.

        What Vestail does
        Names the condition, and asks you to acknowledge it before you buy.

        Cannot own

        The rules exclude someone like you from holding it.

        What Vestail does
        Never routes it. Not even a quote.

        06 Questions

        Fair questions, straight answers.

        Is this legal advice?

        No. Vestail shows what the rules say for the country and buyer type you declare, with sources, so you can decide. For a big or unusual purchase, check with a professional in that country.

        Where do the rules come from?

        From statutes, regulator guidance and issuer terms. Each rule lists its sources and the date it was last verified. During this preview the rules are illustrative and not yet sourced; don't rely on them.

        Does Vestail hold my money?

        Not for onchain purchases: you buy from your own wallet and the asset settles there. Offchain purchases will go through licensed partners, who hold your assets under their own regulation.

        Why can’t I buy something marked “cannot own”?

        Because the rules exclude someone in your position from holding it, and selling it to you anyway is exactly what Vestail exists to prevent. Change your country or buyer type only if it's genuinely true for you.

        How does Vestail make money?

        A small fee on purchases made in Vestail, and referral fees from some venues. Neither ever changes a verdict or which venue we show first.

        Which countries and assets are covered?

        Ten countries and twenty asset classes today, for citizens and foreigners, with residents planned. More countries come as their rules are sourced.
